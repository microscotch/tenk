*Le 10000* is built with Flutter and the open-source libraries below. Thank you to
everyone who writes and maintains them.

## Libraries

### Flutter
The UI toolkit and framework the whole app is built on, by the Flutter Authors and
Google. BSD 3-Clause licence. <https://flutter.dev>

### flutter_localizations
The Flutter SDK's localisation delegates, which translate the framework's own texts
(dates, dialogs, accessibility labels) into the app's languages. Part of Flutter,
BSD 3-Clause licence.

### cupertino_icons
The iOS-style icon font used by Flutter's Cupertino widgets, based on the Framework7
icons by Vladimir Kharlampidi. MIT licence.
<https://github.com/flutter/packages/tree/main/third_party/packages/cupertino_icons>

### flutter_riverpod
Riverpod, by Remi Rousselet: the state management that connects the game engine to the
screens. MIT licence. <https://riverpod.dev>

### flutter_scene
A real-time 3D engine for Flutter by Brandon DeRosier, which draws and rolls the dice.
MIT licence. <https://github.com/bdero/flutter_scene>

### vector_math
Vector and matrix mathematics (2D, 3D and 4D) used to place and rotate the dice in 3D.
By the Flutter team and its contributors. BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/flutter/core-packages/tree/main/packages/vector_math>

### audioplayers
By Blue Fire: plays the dice sounds and the background music, several at once. MIT
licence. <https://github.com/bluefireteam/audioplayers>

### shared_preferences
By the Flutter team: stores the settings (language, sound, delays, theme…) on the
device. BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/flutter/packages/tree/main/packages/shared_preferences/shared_preferences>

### path_provider
By the Flutter team: finds the folders where games and player profiles are saved.
BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/flutter/packages/tree/main/packages/path_provider/path_provider>

### intl
By the Dart team: formats dates and numbers, and powers the translation of the app's
texts into eleven languages. BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/dart-lang/i18n/tree/main/pkgs/intl>

### package_info_plus
By the Flutter Community: reads the version and build number shown in the About
dialog. BSD 3-Clause licence. <https://github.com/fluttercommunity/plus_plugins>

### device_info_plus
By the Flutter Community: tells the game server which kind of phone and system version
is connecting (User-Agent of online games). BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/fluttercommunity/plus_plugins>

### sensors_plus
By the Flutter Community: reads the phone's accelerometer so that shaking it rolls the
dice. BSD 3-Clause licence. <https://github.com/fluttercommunity/plus_plugins>

### share_plus
By the Flutter Community: opens the system share sheet, to invite friends to an online
game and to save a backup file. BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/fluttercommunity/plus_plugins>

### web_socket_channel
By the Dart team: the WebSocket connection to the online game server. BSD 3-Clause
licence. <https://github.com/dart-lang/http/tree/master/pkgs/web_socket_channel>

### app_links
By llfbandit: opens the app on the right room when a friend's invitation link is
tapped. Apache 2.0 licence. <https://github.com/llfbandit/app_links>

### url_launcher
By the Flutter team: opens the store page when an update is available.
BSD 3-Clause licence.
<https://github.com/flutter/packages/tree/main/packages/url_launcher/url_launcher>

### cryptography
By the dint.dev authors: encrypts and decrypts the backup files (AES-256-GCM, with a
key derived from your password by PBKDF2). Apache 2.0 licence.
<https://github.com/dint-dev/cryptography>

### file_picker
By Miguel Ruivo and contributors: lets you choose a backup file to restore. MIT licence.
<https://github.com/vicajilau/flutter_file_picker>

### flutter_markdown_plus
A maintained fork of Flutter's Markdown renderer, which displays this very page.
BSD 3-Clause licence. <https://github.com/foresightmobile/flutter_markdown_plus>

### markdown
By the Dart team: the Markdown parser used by flutter_markdown_plus. BSD 3-Clause
licence. <https://github.com/dart-lang/tools/tree/main/pkgs/markdown>

## Music

on 4 June 2016 (tags: loop, emotional). Released under the CC0 licence (public domain
dedication): no attribution is required, it is given here out of gratitude.
<https://opengameart.org/content/next-to-you> · Author: <https://opengameart.org/users/joth>
